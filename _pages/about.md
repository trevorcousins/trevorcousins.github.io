I am a researcher in the Illumina AI lab. I am interested in using quantitative models and machine learning algorithms to learn about human genetics. Formerly, I was a PhD student in the Department of Genetics at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Professor Richard Durbin and Dr Aylwyn Scally, as well as a visiting researcher in Professor David Reich's lab at Harvard University.
